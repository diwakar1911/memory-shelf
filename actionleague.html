Action League Now!

Details
My notes

I remember real action figures doing funny, somewhat twisted things on Nick. Action League Now! is the one.

Identification

Confirmed as Action League Now!, the toy-based superhero comedy associated with Nickelodeon’s KaBlam!.

About

The League is a group of incompetent superheroes played by physical action figures, dolls, and toys. Their adventures combine miniature figures with real-world objects and deliberately chaotic physical comedy. The familiar team includes The Flesh, Thundergirl, Stinky Diver, and Meltman.

The KaBlam! connection

The shorts appeared within KaBlam!, rather than being a conventional drawn cartoon. Action League Now! was also packaged as a separate programme. The exact episode or broadcast I remember has not been recorded.

Things to recognise

  • Physical toys sharing scenes with everyday objects.
  • The Flesh, Thundergirl, Stinky Diver, and Meltman.
  • Superhero situations going wrong through slapstick accidents.
  • The handmade movement and practical-effects style.
