Details
My notes

A cartoonish, almost egg-shaped car that I drove around a course. The game had a Beetle in it.

Still unidentified. Stunt GP was recognised as a different game and ruled out for this memory. BuzzingCars / Wild Wheels was suggested as a possible match, but has not been identified as this game. The title and its connection to a Digit disc are unconfirmed.

What is known

  • A PC game remembered from the 2000s.
  • The car looked cartoonish and almost egg-shaped.
  • The game included a Beetle.
  • It was driven around a course.

Candidate to compare: BuzzingCars / Wild Wheels

The developer’s site identifies BuzzingCars as the original title of Wild Wheels. It is an Epsitec car game with stylised vehicles and unusual missions.

Possible recognition clues include robots, flying cars, a ghost-car time trial, and missions involving transporting a bomb or retrieving robots from water. The official site describes seven purchasable cars, vehicle parts that break off, and both cockpit and external camera views.

These are facts about the candidate game, not things I have confirmed remembering. Its presence on a Digit disc has not been established.

Ruled out

Stunt GP was recognised as another game I played. It is not the egg-shaped car game.

Still to identify

The actual title, the car, the type of course, and the magazine-disc issue remain open. No pictures are shown until the game is identified.
