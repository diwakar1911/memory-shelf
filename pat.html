Postman Pat.

About

Pat Clifton is the friendly postman in Greendale. His black-and-white cat Jess accompanies him as he delivers the post and helps his neighbours. The red delivery van and Pat’s blue uniform are familiar parts of the show.

Which version?

The remembered title is Postman Pat. The exact series, viewing years, and episodes are not recorded yet. The original series and the later Special Delivery Service are different parts of the franchise; neither has been selected as my specific version.

Things to recognise

  • Pat’s glasses, blue hat, and uniform.
  • Jess riding with him in the red van.
  • Greendale’s village roads and the daily deliveries.
  • The stop-motion characters and small-world scenery.
